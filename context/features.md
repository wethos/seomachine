# Wise Step Recruiting — Services & Differentiators

This document outlines Wise Step's service lines, differentiators, and proof points to inform content that drives qualified inbound (search briefs, discovery calls) rather than trial signups.

"Features" here = **service lines and how we deliver them**. Wise Step is an IT recruitment and headhunting agency (Timișoara, est. 2021), specialised in Cloud & DevOps and Data & AI, serving tech companies across Europe. Positioning: **engineers who recruit**. See `brand-voice.md` and `competitor-analysis.md`.

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. **Engineer-Led Technical Assessment**
- **Service**: Candidates are evaluated on technical depth — stack, role, and what "good" actually looks like — by a co-founder with ~20 years hands-on engineering, not by keyword-matching.
- **Benefit**: Clients get a shortlist that would survive their own technical interview, not CVs that merely contain the right words.
- **Conversion Angle**: "We're former software engineers — we screen for the same things your tech interviewers do."

### 2. **Curated Shortlists, Never CV Floods**
- **Service**: A vetted shortlist of 3–5 candidates per search, each assessed for technical and role fit.
- **Benefit**: Hiring managers review a handful of genuine fits instead of wading through dozens of maybes.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Curated, not bulk — 3–5 vetted engineers, never a CV dump."

### 3. **Deep Specialisation: Cloud/DevOps & Data/AI**
- **Service**: Focused expertise in ML/AI engineers, data engineers, cloud/DevOps/SRE specialists, and technical leadership — the scarce, hard-to-fill roles.
- **Benefit**: In niches where scarcity is high and a wrong hire is expensive, specialisation beats generalist recruiting.
- **Conversion Angle**: "The scarcer the role, the more our specialisation pays off."

### 4. **Headhunting for Passive Leaders**
- **Service**: Direct, confidential outreach to senior and leadership candidates (Engineering Managers, Heads of Data, VP Eng, CTO) who can't be reached through job ads.
- **Benefit**: Access to the people who aren't applying anywhere — the ones job boards can't deliver.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Job ads won't reach the leaders you need. Headhunting will."

### 5. **Fast, Without Trading Quality**
- **Service**: Average 10–15 days to first candidates, 28–30 days to fill — with technical assessment, not despite it.
- **Benefit**: Roles filled quickly enough to matter, without a rushed wrong hire.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Senior roles filled in 28–30 days — quality and speed, not one or the other."

### 6. **Honest Process — No Radio Silence**
- **Service**: Weekly progress reports on active searches, honest feedback, full visibility at every stage. If a search is outside our focus, we say so up front.
- **Benefit**: Clients always know where a search stands and never get sold a clean story that hides the friction.
- **Conversion Angle**: "No surprises, no radio silence — and we only take searches we can genuinely win."

### 7. **AI for Scale, Humans for Judgement**
- **Service**: AI handles sourcing scale and first-pass matching; the founders make the judgement calls on technical and role fit.
- **Benefit**: The reach of automation with the discernment of an engineer — neither replaces the other.
- **Conversion Angle**: "AI handles the scale. Our team handles the judgement."

### 8. **Founder-Run Searches**
- **Service**: The person briefed is the person running the search — work isn't handed down to junior consultants.
- **Benefit**: Continuity and senior attention on every engagement.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Talk to a founder — and that's who runs your search."

## Service Lines

### Placement & Search
- **Permanent Placement**: Full-time hires, mid-level through executive.
- **Executive Search / Headhunting**: Direct, confidential search for senior and leadership roles.
- **Team Build-Out**: Standing up or scaling entire teams — including first offices in Romania.
- **Contract & Freelance**: Contract and freelance technical talent.
- **Retained Search**: Committed, retained engagements for critical or hard-to-fill roles.

### Advisory & Data
- **Market Intelligence**: Salary bands, city-by-city talent density, and market movement data — CEE/Romania tech hiring.

### How We Engage
- **Flexible models**: Retained, contingency, or hybrid — matched to seniority and complexity.
- **Replacement guarantee**: 3–6 months on placements.
- **Fees**: Quoted per engagement, by seniority and complexity. **Internal reference only — typically 15–25% of annual gross; do NOT publish the range in public content. Public framing: "fees quoted per engagement / on request."**

## Recruitment Process

1. **Deep brief** (1–3 days) — understand the role, stack, team, and what "good" looks like
2. **Market search / headhunt** — direct sourcing, including passive candidates
3. **Curated shortlist** — 3–5 vetted candidates
4. **Interviews & technical assessment** — founder-led technical evaluation
5. **Offer structuring & counter-offer management** — including the standard counter-offer nobody warns you about
6. **Onboarding & probation support** — 30–90 days

**Benchmarks:** First candidates in 10–15 days; average time to fill 28–30 days.

### vs. Executive search firms (Evolve Today, HHA, Amrop)
- **Technical depth in the assessment** (they bring search craft, not engineering judgement)
- **Serves mid-level through executive** (pure exec-search firms price out non-C-suite technical hires)
- **Data & AI / DevOps specialisation** (they're industry-generalist or board-level generalist)
- **Fast, transparent, honest process** with weekly reporting

## Common Questions & Objections

### "Do you actually understand the roles you recruit for?"
**Answer**: Yes — a co-founder spent ~20 years in hands-on software engineering and technical leadership. We assess candidates on stack and role depth, the same way your own technical interviewers would.

### "How is this different from a generalist agency?"
**Answer**: We specialise in Cloud/DevOps and Data/AI, we screen on technical depth rather than keywords, and we deliver a curated shortlist of 3–5 — never a CV flood. If a search is outside our focus, we tell you.

### "How fast can you fill a role?"
**Answer**: First candidates typically in 10–15 days, average time to fill 28–30 days — with full technical assessment, not by skipping it.

### "What if the hire doesn't work out?"
**Answer**: Every placement carries a 3–6 month replacement guarantee.

### "What does it cost?"
**Answer**: "Fees are quoted per engagement, based on seniority and complexity, with flexible retained, contingency, and hybrid models." (Do not state the percentage range in public content — it's internal reference only.)

### "Can you help us open in Romania / build a team from scratch?"
**Answer**: Yes — Team Build-Out is a core service, with local market intelligence (salary bands, city-by-city talent density) and honest guidance on the realities, including tax and employment changes.

## Content Creation Guidelines

When writing about Wise Step services:

1. **Lead with the outcome, not the service label** — "a shortlist that survives your tech interview," not "candidate screening"
2. **Back every claim with a number or named fact** — time-to-fill, shortlist size, guarantee length; never an unsourced adjective
3. **Use the proof points** — engineer founders, 28–30 day fill, 3–5 shortlist, 3–6 month guarantee
4. **Address objections proactively** — CV floods, tech literacy, cost of a wrong hire, radio silence
5. **Anti-hype always** — no "game-changing," no emoji-guru energy; if it's exciting but empty, cut it
6. **Match segment to use case** — first office in Romania vs. scaling remote vs. headhunting a leader
7. **Bilingual (EN/RO) and AEO-structured** — write to surface in AI answers on CEE tech-recruitment queries
