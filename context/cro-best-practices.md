# CRO Best Practices for Landing Pages

Conversion Rate Optimization guidelines for Wise Step Recruiting landing pages (wise-step.ro).

Conversion here is **not** a SaaS trial — it's a technology company deciding to brief us on a search, book a discovery call, or request market intelligence. Optimise for qualified conversations, not raw signups.

**Voice constraint (overrides any generic tactic below):** sharp, honest, data-backed, anti-hype. No fake urgency, no countdown timers, no "insane/game-changing" language, no unsourced claims. If a tactic here only works by hyping or manufacturing scarcity, don't use it — an honest, specific picture beats a pretty pitch. See `brand-voice.md`.

## Above-the-Fold Rules

### The 5-Second Test
Visitors should understand these within 5 seconds:
1. **What** — technical recruitment specialised in Cloud/DevOps and Data/AI
2. **Who** — tech companies hiring engineers and technical leaders, mid-level to executive
3. **Why** — engineers who recruit; curated shortlists, ~28–30 day time-to-fill, honest process
4. **What next** — brief a search / book a discovery call

### Required Elements
| Element | Purpose | Guidelines |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Headline | Grab attention | Sharp, specific, anti-hype — a reversal opener works ("used to be X — now it's Y"), <70 chars |
| Subheadline | Clarify value | Back the headline with a concrete number or named fact, 1–2 sentences |
| CTA Button | Drive action | High contrast, action verb, conversation-oriented |
| Trust Signal | Build credibility | Time-to-fill, named client outcome, or engineer-founder proof |

### Above-the-Fold Don'ts
- No sliders or carousels
- No multiple competing CTAs
- No walls of text
- No hype adjectives or emoji-guru energy
- No navigation that distracts from the CTA

## Headline Best Practices

Headlines follow the brand voice: declarative, specific, no fluff. Lead with a number or overturn a lazy assumption.

### Formulas That Fit Our Voice

**Reversal (signature opener):**
- "Romania used to be a cost play. In 2026, it's a speed play."
- "Eastern Europe used to be the cheaper option. Now it's the one that can fill your roles."

**Benchmark-first:**
- "Senior [role] hires, filled in 28–30 days."
- "Curated shortlists of 3–5 vetted engineers — never a CV flood."

**Problem-truth:**
- "Job ads won't reach the leaders you need. Headhunting will."
- "This is not generic HR. It's recruitment by people who've done the engineering."

### Headline Testing Checklist
- [ ] Contains a concrete benefit or number (not an adjective)
- [ ] Under 70 characters (mobile-friendly)
- [ ] Implies the reader ("if you're hiring…")
- [ ] Specific — could only be Wise Step, not a generic agency
- [ ] No hype words, no unsourced claim

### Weak Headlines to Avoid
- "Welcome to Wise Step"
- "The best IT recruitment solution"
- "World-class talent solutions"
- "We're passionate about people"
- Anything with "insane," "game-changing," "revolutionary," or a rocket emoji

## CTA Best Practices

### CTA Text Guidelines

| Goal | Strong CTAs | Avoid |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Brief a search | "Brief your search" / "Start a search" | "Submit" |
| Discovery call | "Book a discovery call" / "Talk to a founder" | "Contact us" |
| Market intelligence | "Get the salary benchmarks" / "Download the 2026 report" | "Learn more" |
| Candidate side | "See open roles" / "Join the network" | "Register" |

Note: "Talk to a founder" is a genuine differentiator — the founders run searches directly. Use it where true.

### CTA Button Formula
**[Action Verb] + [Benefit/Object]**

Examples:
- "Book" + "a discovery call"
- "Brief" + "your search"
- "Get" + "the 2026 salary benchmarks"

### CTA Placement Strategy
1. **Hero CTA** (0–20% of page): primary, most prominent
2. **Post-problem CTA** (30–40%): after establishing the hiring pain (scarcity, slow/expensive wrong hire)
3. **Post-proof CTA** (60–70%): after outcomes/testimonials
4. **Closing CTA** (90–100%): final push with the replacement-guarantee risk reversal

### CTA Visual Best Practices
- **Color**: high contrast with background
- **Size**: tappable (44×44px minimum)
- **Whitespace**: breathing room around the button
- **Consistency**: same style throughout
- **Mobile**: full-width on small screens

## Trust Signal Hierarchy

Our credibility is built on concrete facts, not adjectives. Every signal below must be sourced or true.

### Strongest (Use First)
1. **Specific outcomes**: "Filled a Head of Data search in 26 days" / "3–5 vetted candidates, not a CV flood"
2. **Engineer-founder proof**: "Founded by engineers — ~20 years hands-on before we recruited a single hire"
3. **Named client outcomes**: real company + real result (with permission)

### Strong
4. **Process benchmarks**: "First candidates in 10–15 days, average fill in 28–30"
5. **Replacement guarantee**: "3–6 month replacement guarantee on every placement"
6. **Specialisation proof**: named stacks/roles we've placed (ML/AI, data eng, DevOps/SRE, VP Eng/CTO)

### Supporting
7. **Engagement flexibility**: retained, contingency, or hybrid
8. **Market intelligence**: our own salary/talent-density data as evidence we know the market
9. **Honesty signal**: "If a search is outside our focus, we say so"

### Trust Signal Placement
| Location | Best Trust Signal |
|----------|-------------------|
| Hero (above fold) | Time-to-fill benchmark or engineer-founder proof |
| After problem section | A specific placement story with the outcome |
| Middle of page | 2–3 named client outcomes |
| Near CTA | Replacement guarantee + "we only take searches we can win" |
| Footer | Company details, honest process statement |

## Risk Reversal Tactics

Recruitment risk reversal is about de-risking a wrong or slow hire — the thing that's genuinely expensive for the client.

### For Briefing a Search
- "3–6 month replacement guarantee"
- "Curated shortlist of 3–5 — you never wade through CV floods"
- "We only take searches we can genuinely win — if it's outside our focus, we tell you"
- "Flexible engagement: retained, contingency, or hybrid"

### For a Discovery Call
- "No hard sell — a straight read on whether we can fill this"
- "Just 20–30 minutes"
- "Honest market view, even if the answer is 'this'll be hard'"

### For Market Intelligence / Lead Magnet
- "Real salary bands and talent density, sourced — not vibes"
- "No spam, no radio silence"
- "Instant download"

### Placement
Put risk reversal **directly below or beside the CTA**. The replacement guarantee is the single strongest reversal — lead with it near the closing CTA.

## Objection Handling

### Common Objections & Responses (Recruitment)

**"Agencies just send CV floods."**
- Lead with "curated shortlist of 3–5 vetted candidates, never bulk"
- Show the screening depth — we assess on technical, not keywords

**"Recruiters don't understand the tech."**
- "We're former software engineers — ~20 years hands-on"
- "We evaluate on technical depth and what 'good' actually looks like"

**"It's too expensive."**
- Frame against the cost of a wrong or slow senior hire
- Show the 3–6 month replacement guarantee (fee is protected)
- Explain flexible models (retained / contingency / hybrid), 15–25% by seniority/complexity

**"We can't reach the people we need."**
- That's the headhunting case — "job ads won't reach passive leaders; we will"

**"How do I know you can actually fill this?"**
- Specialisation proof (Cloud/DevOps, Data/AI, technical leadership)
- Time-to-fill benchmarks (10–15 days to first candidates, 28–30 to fill)
- Honesty: "if we can't add value, we say so up front"

## Form Optimization

### Field Reduction
Every extra field costs conversions. But a search brief legitimately needs more than an email — signal *why* you're asking.

**Recommended Fields by Goal:**
| Goal | Fields | What to Ask |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Discovery call | 3–4 | Name, work email, company, role you're hiring |
| Brief a search | 4–6 | Above + stack/seniority, brief note (qualifies + speeds the brief) |
| Market intelligence | 1–2 | Work email, (company) |

### Form Best Practices
- Single column layout
- Clear labels above fields
- Helpful placeholder text (e.g. "e.g. Senior DevOps, Timișoara or remote")
- Real-time validation
- Explain longer forms: "The more you tell us, the sharper the first shortlist"

### Form Placement
- Above the fold for paid traffic
- After value establishment for SEO/AEO content
- Never buried at the very bottom alone

## Page Speed Impact

Site runs on Astro + Cloudflare Pages — lean by default. Keep it that way.

### Conversion Impact
- 1 second delay ≈ 7% conversion drop
- 3 second load ≈ 40% abandonment
- Mobile users expect <3 seconds

### Quick Wins
- Compress images (WebP); Canva exports optimised
- Minimise JavaScript (Astro ships little by default — don't add heavy client JS)
- Lazy-load below-fold assets
- System/optimised fonts
- Leverage Cloudflare caching

## Mobile Optimization

### Mobile-First Rules
- Design for mobile, then adapt for desktop
- Touch-friendly tap targets (44×44px)
- Readable without zooming (16px+ font)

### Mobile CTA Guidelines
- Full-width buttons
- Sticky CTA on scroll (optional)
- Click-to-call / click-to-email for direct founder contact
- Simplified forms

### Mobile Content Rules
- Shorter headlines (<60 chars)
- Front-load the number/benefit
- Accordion FAQs to save space
- Minimal navigation

## Bilingual (EN/RO) Considerations

All landing pages ship in English and Romanian.
- Translate the *pitch*, not just the words — the sharp, anti-hype voice must survive.
- Keep stack names, role titles, and benchmarks identical across languages.
- Mirror CTA wording and form structure across both versions.
- Serve SEO **and** AEO — structure so pages surface in AI-generated answers to Romanian/CEE tech-recruitment queries.

## A/B Testing Priorities

Measurement via Google Search Console + conversion tracking. One variable at a time.

### High Impact (Test First)
1. Headline (reversal vs. benchmark-first)
2. CTA text ("Book a discovery call" vs. "Talk to a founder" vs. "Brief your search")
3. CTA color/size
4. Hero proof (time-to-fill vs. engineer-founder vs. named outcome)
5. Trust-signal placement

### Medium Impact
6. Subheadline
7. Form length (short vs. qualifying)
8. Which client outcomes shown
9. Fee-model framing
10. Risk-reversal wording (replacement guarantee prominence)

### Low Impact (Test Later)
11. Button shape
12. Font choices
13. Footer content
14. Image style
15. Color scheme

### Testing Rules
- One variable at a time
- Given B2B recruitment volumes, conversions accrue slowly — judge on qualified conversations, not just clicks; don't call a test early
- Run long enough for a real sample before deciding

## Psychology Principles

Use these **only** where honest. Our anti-hype standard bars manufactured scarcity and fake urgency outright.

### Authority (our strongest lever)
- Engineer founders (~20 years hands-on)
- Named stacks/roles placed
- Our own market data (salary bands, talent density)
- First-hand, sourced takes on real market movement

### Social Proof
- Named client outcomes with real results
- Specific placement stories
- Testimonials that speak to technical depth, not just "great to work with"

### Reciprocity
- Free market intelligence / salary benchmarks before any ask
- Genuinely useful, sourced content

### Real Scarcity & Urgency — HANDLE WITH CARE
- Real capacity limits are fine to state plainly ("we run a limited number of searches at once")
- **Never** fabricate countdowns, fake spot-counters, or "act now" pressure — it violates the brand voice and reads as the generic-agency hype we reject
- "Move with urgency, but never fake it"

## Landing Page Audit Checklist

### Above the Fold
- [ ] Sharp, specific, anti-hype headline
- [ ] Value clear in 5 seconds (what/who/why/next)
- [ ] Prominent, conversation-oriented CTA
- [ ] Concrete trust signal visible (number or named fact)
- [ ] No hype, no distracting elements

### Content
- [ ] Benefits/outcomes before features
- [ ] Scannable (lists, tables, bold, headers) — playbook-shaped
- [ ] Every claim sourced or backed by a number
- [ ] Addresses recruitment objections (CV floods, tech literacy, cost, reach)
- [ ] Clear next step

### CTAs
- [ ] Action verb + object
- [ ] High contrast
- [ ] Distributed through the page
- [ ] Goal-aligned messaging
- [ ] Replacement-guarantee / honesty risk reversal nearby

### Trust
- [ ] Named client outcomes with real results
- [ ] Specific benchmarks (time-to-fill, shortlist size)
- [ ] Engineer-founder proof
- [ ] Replacement guarantee present
- [ ] "We only take searches we can win" honesty signal

### Technical
- [ ] Mobile responsive
- [ ] Fast load (<3s on Astro/Cloudflare)
- [ ] Forms work correctly
- [ ] EN + RO versions both live and mirrored
- [ ] Analytics / Search Console tracking installed

## Quick Reference: Conversion Killers

**Avoid These:**
1. Generic headlines ("Welcome to our site," "world-class solutions")
2. Weak CTAs ("Submit," "Click here")
3. Unsourced claims or invented figures — instant credibility kill for us
4. Hype adjectives / emoji-guru energy
5. Fake urgency or countdown timers
6. Walls of unformatted text
7. Multiple competing goals
8. Forms with too many *unexplained* fields
9. No risk reversal (no replacement guarantee visible)
10. "Family" / corporate-fluff tone that could belong to any agency
